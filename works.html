Project

Reviving the mammoth steppe

Description

This infographics was part of a series done in collaboration with Ilustraciencia and the climate scientist Fernando Valladares about the climate change tipping points. One of these, the defrosting of permafrost, would liberate massive amounts of greenhouse gases, greatly worsening global warming. Restoring the populations of large herbivores that populated the boreal steppes at the ice age once could prevent this. To try to confer the paleo feeling, I went for loose brushstrokes, earthy textures and a warm palette.

Project

A portrait of the Caribou

Description

A naturalist portrait of the caribou, part of a series of illustrations on key species facing the pressures of climate change, developed during a scientific illustration course given by Ilustraciencia. The main portrait was built with graphite and watercolour, later refined digitally, and paired with a set of pencil studies —the animal grazing and a detail of its snow-adapted hooves— assembled together into a species fact sheet.

Project

The power of macroalgae. A biological solution for polluted coasts

Description

Coastal environments are subject to a constant polluting pressure from human activities. This is particularly intense in urban cities with large harbours and intense marine traffic. In Mediterranean Algae, they take advantage of the bioremediation capacities of macroalgae to counter this, while deploying a cloud-based platform for real time monitoring of water quality parameters for informed decision making.
