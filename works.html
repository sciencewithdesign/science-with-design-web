A portrait of the caribou

A naturalist illustration of a caribou. Created with traditional methods, combining graphite, water colour and later digital refinement. It belonged to a series of illustrations centered around key representative species of the struggle that climate change poses on biodiversity. Developed during a course of scientific illustration, given by Ilustraciencia.
